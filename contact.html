Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often. Still stuck? Just reach out.

How do I know which helmet size to order?
Measure your head circumference just above the eyebrows. Match it to our sizing guide — if you're between sizes, always go up. Still unsure? Contact us and we'll help.
What is your return & exchange policy?
We accept returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery — as long as items are unworn and in original packaging. Helmets must have all tags and visor film intact.
Do you offer international shipping?
Yes — we ship to over 30 countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times are calculated at checkout based on your location and order size.
Can I try gear in-store before buying?
Absolutely. Visit any of our three California locations to try on helmets, jackets, gloves, and boots. Our staff will help you find the perfect fit.
